Frequently Asked Questions

What is your quality guarantee?
Your satisfaction is our absolute top priority. We guarantee top-tier quality for every pair in our catalogue. If you are unhappy with your order for any reason, we make it incredibly easy to get a refund or an exchange.
How does Delivery and COD work?

We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for clients based around Johannesburg. You can pay via cash or immediate EFT upon delivery once you have inspected your pair.

For the rest of SA, standard nationwide delivery via Courier Guy (to your door or a PUDO locker) takes 2-4 working days once full payment clears.

Can I return or exchange my shoes?
Yes! Like we said, your satisfaction is guaranteed. We fully support refunds and size exchanges within 30 days of receiving your order, as long as the sneakers are unworn and in their original packaging. Standard shipping fees apply.
